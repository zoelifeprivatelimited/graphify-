## IA-01 · IAOMAI · Carousel · Instagram + Facebook · Mon 23 Nov, 19:30 IST

**Hook (slide 1):** Wedding season. Steady sugar.

**Slides**
1. Wedding season. Steady sugar. 5 small habits for mithai-heavy weeks.
2. Fill half your plate with salad and sabzi first.
3. Choose one sweet you love. Skip the ones you don't.
4. Take a 10-minute walk after the big meal. Dance counts.
5. Keep taking your prescribed medicines on time, even on busy days.
6. Celebrate well. Check in with your doctor about your numbers.

**Caption**
Over 10 crore Indians live with diabetes, and wedding season is one of the hardest times of the year to eat well.
A few small habits help you enjoy the celebrations and stay on track:
• Start with salad and sabzi
• Pick one sweet you really love
• Walk for 10 minutes after the big meal
• Never skip your prescribed medicines
Save this for your next shaadi.

**CTA:** Save this post and share it with someone attending a wedding this week.

**Hashtags:** #IAOMAI #WeddingSeason #BloodSugar #HealthyHabits #DiabetesAwareness #MindfulEating #IndianWeddings #WellnessIndia #HealthyIndia

**Design brief:** cobalt blue on white, one line per slide, a small illustration of a thali or mithai box on slide 1. No product shot.

**Compliance note**
- "Over 10 crore Indians live with diabetes": ICMR-INDIAB study, Anjana RM et al., *The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology*, 2023 (101 million people). https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(23)00119-5
- Tips are general lifestyle advice, not treatment claims. Portion and sugar advice is in line with ICMR-NIN *Dietary Guidelines for Indians* (2024). https://www.nin.res.in/dietaryguidelines/
- No product claim. No cure, treatment or guarantee language.
- FLAG FOR ABEL: "Walk for 10 minutes after the big meal" is general advice; keep it or drop it?
